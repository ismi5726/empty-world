This program is an extension of the previous one, this time using marching cubes to render a full terrain, rather than just a few metaballs. Controls are as follows:

Full controls list:
- scroll to zoom in / out
- m to change between the perspectives (ortho, perspective, walking)
- 0 to reset scene
- q / esc to exit
normal controls:
- click and drag to rotate the camera around the viewing point
walking controls:
- wasd to move
- space to jump
- click and drag to turn camera

Time taken: 8 hours, a lot of which was used to solve various problems (how to represent terrain with arbitrary coordinates without the ease of objects from javascript, for instance). The dodecahedrons may be clipping into each other, sorry.
